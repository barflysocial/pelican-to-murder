Rules:

- No repeated suspect names across episodes unless intentionally recurring.
- No reused evidence across episodes.
- No repeated culprit/method/motive outcomes.

- Forensics is for analysis of evidence after it is sent for processing.
- Interrogation is for suspects, witnesses, alibis, and follow-up prompts.
